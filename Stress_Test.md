### Stress Test Strategy

In this strategy, testers play the game doing the opposite of the tips, covering outputs, paths and interactions outside the set progression.

### Objectives

- Do the opposite of the tips.
    - Does the game offer any tips or guidance to outputs?
    - Does the game offer any tips or guidance to paths?
    - Does the game offer any tips or guidance to NPCs?
- Check and cover other outputs, paths and interactions.
    - Does the game specify the controls?
    - Is the game progression path set?
    - Is the game progression with NPCs set?

### Examples

- The game tells you to pick up a key to open a door. Leave the key behind and try to open the door anyway, or carry the key somewhere else.
- The game specifies a button to interact with an object. Press other buttons near the object to check for unexpected triggers.
- The level progresses from left to right. Go back to the left or try other directions to see how the game reacts.
- An NPC is supposed to be talked to before being fought. Attack it first, or interact with it in a different order.
